Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about turnaround times, sending raw 4K footage, and working together.

What is your turnaround time for YouTube videos and Shorts?
▼

Standard turnaround for a polished 10–15 minute YouTube edit is 48 to 72 hours. Vertical Shorts / Reels batches are delivered in 24–48 hours. Rush delivery (24-hour turnaround) is available on request.

How do I send my raw camera footage and audio files?
▼

You can upload files to Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or Frame.io. I also support direct S3 bucket transfers and private FTP servers for high-volume 4K ProRes/RAW multi-terabyte shoots.

How do revisions work if I want changes made?
▼

All projects include 2 full rounds of timestamped revisions via Frame.io or Loom links. You can leave comments directly on the exact video frame for swift, zero-misunderstanding adjustments.

Can you edit in the style of MrBeast, Iman Gadzhi, or MagnatesMedia?
▼

Yes! I specialize in reverse-engineering top creator styles including high-energy fast cuts (MrBeast/Airrack), cinematic documentary style (MagnatesMedia/Johnny Harris), and modern talking-head aesthetic (Iman Gadzhi/Ali Abdaal).
